Click on the buttons in the left side bar, the images in the grid, or press Shift + ( 1 - 9 ) to select a player.

Now you can edit the player's color.

Select the same player again or press Esc to go back to the full preview.

Your changes will be reflected on the url, so you can save it as a bookmark in your browser or easily share it.

To use your palette in game, click on the button on the bottom left, to get the file spritecolors.json.

Place this file in your_game_install_folder\resources\_common\palettes .

I'd recommend making a backup of the original before.

You can even do this while the game is running, but changes won't be reflected until you load a game again.

You can see this tutorial anytime by pressing the ? button on the sidebar or the F1 key in your keyboard.
