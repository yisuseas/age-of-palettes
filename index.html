Click on the colored buttons in the left side bar, the images in the grid, or press Shift + ( 1 - 9 ) to select a player, this will open their list of properties.

      Select the same player again or press Esc to go back to the full preview.

      Click on any of the player's properties to open the color editor, where you can modify that specific color.

      Your changes will not only be reflected on the preview to the right, but also on the url, so you can save it as a bookmark in your browser or easily share it.

      To use your palette in game, click on the button on the bottom left, this will download MyPalette.zip. Extract its contents to your local mods folder: C:\Users\USERNAME\Games\Age of Empires 2 DE\BIG_NUMBER\mods\local

      You can see this tutorial anytime by pressing the ? button on the sidebar or the F1 key in your keyboard.
